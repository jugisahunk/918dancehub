# Reveal the Venue's real address early, instead of waiting for Grand Opening

The site launched with the `Midtown Tulsa Adjacent` policy (CONTEXT.md): no exact street address anywhere until Grand Opening (2026-10-10), to avoid premature foot traffic while the Venue was being prepared. During Google Business Profile setup (issue #24), Christina decided to reveal the real address everywhere immediately instead. The address was already public on the Business Profile listing (Google's category for this business type expects a visible location) and on Facebook, so continuing to withhold it on the website no longer avoided anything — it just left the site inconsistent with what customers could already find elsewhere.

Address: 2732 S Memorial Dr, Tulsa, OK 74129.

This retires the `Midtown Tulsa Adjacent` glossary entry (see CONTEXT.md) and supersedes issue #26 ("Flip Grand Opening reveal live"), which was scoped to make this exact change on 2026-10-10 — that work now happens now, under issue #28, instead.

## Considered Options

- **Wait for Grand Opening as originally planned** — rejected: the address was no longer actually secret once it was public on Google/Facebook, so the wait no longer served the "avoid premature foot traffic" goal it was designed for.
- **Hide the address on the website only, leave GBP/Facebook as-is** — rejected: would leave the site visibly out of step with what's already public elsewhere, which reads as an oversight rather than a deliberate choice.
